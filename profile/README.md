# OpenReefData

**OpenReefData (ORD)** aims to build an open-source ecosystem of schemas, registries, and utilities for **standardizing coral reef research and restoration data**.

Preserving our planet's coral reefs is inherently a community effort requiring unprecedented collaboration and cooperation. To make that possible, we need our tools and the data they produce to work together. 

The goal of ORD is to provide an **open, community-governed data foundation** that empowers individuals and organizations to build, modify, and connect tools for their specific needs while still allowing them to effortlessly share their data and custom solutions with others.

---

## Why OpenReefData?

Coral reef restoration practitioners collect rich, interconnected data: genets, fragments, cohorts, nurseries, outplants, mortality, growth, and more.  
But today those data are:

- siloed in spreadsheets and custom databases
- modeled inconsistently across organizations
- hard to validate, translate, or interoperate

ORD exists to solve the *infrastructure* problem:

> Let tools innovate rapidly — but let the **data model itself** be stable, explicit, and shared.

---

## What’s in this organization?

This GitHub organization hosts the **core building blocks** of the ORD ecosystem:

### [📐 `ord-schema`](https://github.com/OpenReefData/ord-schema)
Canonical, versioned **JSON Schemas** defining ORD data models  
(e.g. species, genets, locations, cohorts, events)

### [📚 `ord-registry`](https://github.com/OpenReefData/ord-registry)
Machine-readable **registries** of controlled identifiers used by ORD schemas  
(e.g. species codes, shared genets, organization IDs, enumerations)

### [🐙 `ord-tools`](https://github.com/OpenReefData/ord-tools)
Deployed reference applications (web portal, mobile app) showing the ecosystem in action
